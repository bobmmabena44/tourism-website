Welcome to Lesotho

Discover a Different Side of Africa

Lesotho is a mountainous kingdom completely surrounded by South Africa. Its dramatic highlands, distinctive traditions and historic landmarks make it a unique destination for travellers seeking nature, culture and adventure.

From winding mountain roads to traditional villages and powerful waterfalls, every region offers an opportunity to discover something new.

Lesotho Horizons brings these experiences together to help you explore the country's attractions, understand its heritage and prepare for your journey.

Nature and Engineering

Katse Dam

Set among the highlands, Katse Dam is one of Lesotho's major engineering landmarks and part of the Lesotho Highlands Water Project. The surrounding mountain scenery makes it a memorable place to explore.

Waterfalls and Adventure

Maletsunyane Falls

Located near Semonkong, Maletsunyane Falls plunges into a deep gorge surrounded by rugged scenery. It is a striking destination for sightseeing, photography and outdoor adventure.

History and Heritage

Thaba Bosiu

Thaba Bosiu is closely associated with King Moshoeshoe I and the establishment of the Basotho nation. It is an important place to explore the history and heritage of Lesotho.

The Heart of Lesotho

A Living Culture with a Proud Heritage

Lesotho's identity is rooted in the traditions, history and community life of the Basotho people. Traditional clothing, music, crafts and ceremonies remain important expressions of cultural heritage.

The Basotho blanket is a distinctive part of traditional dress, while the mokorotlo, a traditional conical hat, is a recognised national symbol.

Exploring Lesotho is not only about seeing beautiful landscapes. It is also about learning the stories of its people and respecting their customs.

Travel with Confidence

Plan Before You Explore

Use these practical reminders to prepare for a safer and more enjoyable visit.

Prepare for Mountain Weather

Temperatures can vary considerably with elevation and season. Pack warm clothing, comfortable walking shoes and rain protection.

Check Your Travel Route

Research road conditions and transport options before visiting remote destinations. Allow additional time for mountain journeys.

Respect Culture and Nature

Ask permission before photographing people, follow local guidance and help protect the environment by disposing of waste responsibly.

Use Reliable Information

Check current entry requirements, weather, transport and attraction details before setting out on your trip.
